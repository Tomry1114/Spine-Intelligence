At a glance

ViT3Flow explores how patient-specific postoperative radiographic appearance can be synthesized from a preoperative whole-spine radiograph. It combines case-adaptive token modeling with morphology-guided anatomical retrieval in a single-evaluation MeanFlow framework.

Research framework. Model outputs are not a substitute for clinical judgment.
