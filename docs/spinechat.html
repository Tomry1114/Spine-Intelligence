At a glance

SpineChat studies progressive spinal deformity assessment: surface RGBD observations provide initial screening evidence, followed by radiographs for structural evaluation. The framework connects spatial evidence, curve-specific grounding, and reliability-aware clinical reasoning.

Research framework. Model outputs are not a substitute for clinical judgment.
